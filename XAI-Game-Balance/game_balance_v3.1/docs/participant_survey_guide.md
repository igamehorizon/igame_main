# Playtester Guide — Leaving Feedback (Section 06)

Thank you for taking part in this playtest. After the tool analyzes your
gameplay data, you'll see a section near the bottom of the screen called
**"06 // LEAVE A REVIEW"**. This short guide explains what it's for and how
to fill it in.

## When to fill it in

**Fill out Section 06 after every analysis you run — not just once at the
end.**

That means:
- After your **first** analysis (the baseline), fill it in.
- If you then apply any of the suggested changes and run a **follow-up**
  analysis, fill it in again for that follow-up.
- If there's a second follow-up, fill it in again, and so on.

Each time, the ratings and comments should reflect **only what you just
saw** — the specific recommendations and results from that run, not the
whole session. This lets us see whether your experience changes after
suggestions are applied, which is the main thing we're trying to learn.

## What the ratings mean

You'll see five questions on a 1–5 scale (1 = strongly disagree,
5 = strongly agree):

1. **"The workflow was clear and easy to follow."** — Did you understand
   what to do at each step, without needing help?
2. **"The recommendations felt useful and actionable."** — Did the
   suggestions feel like things you could actually use?
3. **"The explanations were understandable."** — When the tool told you
   *why* it made a suggestion, did that reasoning make sense?
4. **"I could see myself applying these suggestions in development."** —
   Realistically, could you picture using this suggestion in your own
   work?
5. **"Overall, I would use this tool again."** — Your general impression.

Below the ratings there's also an open text box for anything else you'd
like to add — specific confusions, things you liked, things you'd change.
Both the ratings and the text box are there to help; you can fill in just
the ratings, just the text, or both, but at least one is needed to submit.

## Privacy

No names, emails, or identifying information are collected by the tool.
Please avoid including your own name or contact details in the written
comment box, just to be safe.

- [ ] Rated what I just saw, not the whole session
- [ ] Added a comment if I have something to say
- [ ] Submitted before moving on
- [ ] Repeated this after every follow-up iteration
